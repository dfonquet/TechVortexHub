01 / What I do

Networks with purpose.
Engineering driven by curiosity.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of Service Provider and enterprise networking, including BGP, MPLS, SD-WAN, cloud connectivity, security platforms, and multi-vendor environments across Cisco, Juniper, Fortinet, and Meraki.

I like to go beyond simply making a configuration work. I want to understand why a route was selected, how a service behaves end to end, how a migration should be designed and executed, and how automation can make network operations more predictable and reliable.

More recently, I have been exploring how AI can support engineering workflows, from research and troubleshooting to documentation, automation, validation, and better-informed technical decisions.

I’m still the same curious engineer who builds things in the lab, breaks them, fixes them, documents what happened, and learns something new in the process.
