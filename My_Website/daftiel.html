01 / Behind the configuration

Networks with purpose.
Engineering with curiosity.

Most of my work lives between Service Provider and enterprise environments: MPLS VPNs, BGP, SD-WAN, cloud connectivity, security platforms, and multi-vendor infrastructure across Cisco, Juniper, Fortinet, Meraki, and related technologies.

I like going beyond the configuration line. I care about why a route is selected, how a service is validated end to end, how a migration should be sequenced, and how automation can make operations cleaner and more predictable.

I am still that curious engineer who breaks things in the lab, rebuilds them with patience, documents the lesson, and keeps learning with purpose.
